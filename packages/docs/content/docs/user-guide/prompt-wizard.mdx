## Overview

After creating your brand, Elmo launches the **Prompt Wizard** — an AI-powered tool that analyzes your website and generates relevant prompts for tracking. These prompts are the questions Elmo will ask AI models to monitor your brand's visibility.

## Wizard Steps

The wizard walks you through five stages:

### 1. Analyze Your Website

Elmo visits your website and extracts information about your products, services, and offerings.

Research uses a limited set of at most six public official pages, discovered
from the supplied page's real navigation links. It is not a full-site scan.
Each page has one shared budget of at most three HTTP requests, including
reader/direct-fetch switching and redirects. Recoverable failures wait before
retrying; successful pages are not fetched again. If no page yields material,
the website-dependent research stops without a model request.

### 2. Discover Keywords

Based on the analysis, Elmo generates relevant keywords that people might search for when looking for products or services like yours.

You can review and adjust the suggested keywords before continuing.

### 3. Identify Competitors

Elmo suggests competitors based on your products and industry. These are the brands you want to track alongside your own in AI responses.

Add, remove, or edit competitors as needed.

### 4. Define Personas

Elmo creates buyer personas that represent the types of people who would search for your products. Different personas may trigger different AI responses.

{/* TODO: Screenshot of the personas step */}

### 5. Generate Prompts

Combining keywords, competitors, and personas, Elmo generates a set of tracking prompts. Each prompt is a question that will be asked to AI models.

{/* TODO: Screenshot of the prompt generation step */}

Review the generated prompts and select the ones you want to track. You can also add custom prompts.

Automatically included suggestions must be unbranded. Product, series and model
terms are assessed in the same research response, regardless of which SKU field
contains them. A judgement can be used only when its source URL belongs to the
supplied crawled pages and its quote matches that page and names the term.
Source verification confirms the quote, not proprietary rights or the accuracy
of the model's semantic judgement.

Suggestions containing unresolved terms or conflicting assessments remain
unconfirmed rather than being silently accepted as generic. Original assessments
and source-check results are retained in the research draft. Missing fields in
older responses do not discard the rest of the research, rewrite saved prompts,
or trigger another model call; review doubtful suggestions manually.

Every generated question must have non-empty text in `prompt`. Missing text,
empty text, invalid rows, duplicates and target-reference exclusions are
reported separately; a completed job with no usable questions is not a complete
research success. Valid rows retain their wording and casing. No default
questions are added, and model failures do not trigger an automatic retry.

Review distinguishes business sectors, product lines and concrete SKUs.
A category with a checked official source quote may have no verified SKU.
An unsupported SKU remains pending without discarding its sourced category or
other verified SKUs. Unknown parent relationships are not invented.
Hierarchy, source URLs and verbatim quotes survive draft editing, confirmation
and profile reload. Historical records without these fields remain readable as
unclassified or pending; they are not researched again.

Layer names remain model suggestions until reviewed. An authentic quote can
describe a sub-brand rather than a product line. Marking a suggested role as
uncertain keeps it pending without removing valid questions or other categories.

Address safety checks reject private, loopback and benchmark/fake-IP destinations.
When an explicitly authorized isolated deployment needs an encrypted DNS route,
`WEBSITE_RESEARCH_DNS_OVER_HTTPS=1.1.1.1` uses that public resolver with TLS
verification and checked-address pinning. It does not change system DNS or
exempt private addresses. DNS queries are cached per host, bounded to fourteen
per research and recorded separately from website HTTP requests.

## After the Wizard

Once you confirm the prompts, Elmo marks your brand as onboarded and you're taken to the main dashboard. Saving and viewing the result do not authorize a new research, sampling or model call; monitoring is started explicitly.

You can always add, edit, or disable prompts later in **Settings → Prompts**.

## Manual Prompt Creation

If you prefer to skip the wizard or want to add prompts later, go to **Settings → Prompts** and add them manually. Each prompt needs:

- **Text** — The question to ask AI models
- **Tags** — Optional tags for filtering and organization
- **Enabled** — Toggle tracking on or off for individual prompts
